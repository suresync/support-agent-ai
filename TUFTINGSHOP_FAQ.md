## Orders & shipping

### How long does shipping take?
We normally ship within **1–2 business days** after your order is placed. During busy periods there can be a short delay. Once your order ships, you receive tracking details by email. You can also check status in our Help Center.

### How can I track my order?
Use the tracking link/number in your shipment email, or log in to the Help Center with your order details. If tracking has not updated for several days, reply to your order email with your order number and we will check with the carrier (often DPD or DHL).

### My tracking has not moved / is stuck in the Netherlands. What should I do?
Carrier delays do happen (weather, depot congestion, international handoff). Contact us with your order number and tracking number. We will open a tracer with the carrier and update you when we have news.

### Tracking says delivered, but I did not receive my package. What now?
Please:
1. Check around the house, safe places, and with neighbors (parcels are sometimes left next door without notice).
2. Confirm the delivery address and ZIP/postcode on your order.
3. Contact us with your order number so we can share the carrier’s proof of delivery and investigate further if needed.

### Will my order arrive in more than one box?
Larger orders (especially yarn cones) are often split into **multiple parcels**, each with its own tracking number. If you received only one box, wait for the other tracking updates or ask us to confirm how many packages were shipped.

### Can I change my delivery address after ordering?
Address changes are only possible **before the order ships**. Contact us as soon as possible with the correct address and order number. Once the parcel is with the carrier, we usually cannot change the destination.

### Can I change items in my order (colors, sizes, quantities)?
If the order has **not shipped yet**, we can often swap items or adjust quantities. Email us quickly with your order number and the exact change you want. After shipping, changes are no longer possible.

## Customs, taxes & international shipping

### Does the shipping fee include customs / tariffs / import charges?
**No.** The shipping fee covers transport only. Customs duties, VAT, or import fees may be charged by your country’s customs authority when the parcel arrives (especially outside the EU). These are the customer’s responsibility.

### I did not expect customs charges. Can I cancel?
If the order has **not shipped**, contact us immediately and we can usually cancel and refund. If it has already shipped, cancellation may no longer be possible—contact us as soon as you can so we can check.

## Cancellations, returns & refunds

### Can I cancel my order?
Yes, **as long as it has not shipped**. Email us with your order number right away. Once shipped, we cannot stop the parcel in transit in most cases.

### Can I return tufting cloth / primary backing?
**Cut-to-order cloth generally cannot be returned**, because it is cut specifically for your order. See our full [refund policy](https://tuftingshop.com/policies/refund-policy). If you ordered the wrong width or size, contact us **before shipping** so we can swap it.

### I ordered the wrong cloth size (e.g. 1×4 m instead of 2×2 m). What can you do?
Contact us immediately with the order number. If the order has not shipped, we can usually replace the item and refund any price difference.

### When will I receive my refund?
Refunds are issued to your **original payment method**. Bank transfers can take several business days to appear. If it has been longer than expected, reply with your order number and we will verify the transfer.

## Missing, incomplete, or wrong items

### Something is missing from my order. What should I do?
Email us with:
- Order number  
- Which item(s) and quantities are missing  
- Photos of what you received (and the packing list, if available)

We will check whether the order was split across packages or if there was a packing error. Typical resolutions: **reship the missing items**, **refund those items**, or a **store voucher**—we’ll offer options based on your preference.

### I received the wrong items. What now?
Contact us with your order number and a short description (or photo) of what arrived versus what you ordered. We will arrange a correction as quickly as possible.

### My package only contained part of my order (e.g. cloth but no guns / clippers).
This can be a split shipment or a warehouse packing error. Reach out with your order number; if items are confirmed missing, we prioritize reshipping them.

## Discounts & vouchers

### My discount / promo code does not work at checkout. What should I do?
Double-check:
- The code is entered exactly as provided  
- You are logged in with the **same email** the voucher was issued to  
- The code has not expired and applies to the products in your cart  

If it still fails, email us with a screenshot and we will fix or reissue the code.

### I missed a sale / Black Friday discount on my order. Can you apply it afterward?
Sale discounts usually apply only during the promotion window. In some cases we can issue a **personal voucher** for the missed discount value on a future order—contact support with your order number.

## Products & getting started

### Which tufting cloth / yarn / machine should I buy as a beginner?
Tell us your experience level, budget, and what you want to make (rugs, wall hangings, etc.). Our team can recommend a starter setup. For specific product questions (cloth width, yarn colors, machine cut vs loop), include links to the product pages when you email us.

### Can I get a B2B or workshop discount for regular orders?
Workshops and frequent buyers can request a personal discount arrangement. Email us with your business/workshop details and expected order frequency.

## How to contact us

Email **info@tuftingshop.com** (or reply to any order email) and include:
1. Your **order number**  
2. A short description of the issue  
3. Tracking number and photos if relevant  

If you receive an automatic reply that does not answer your question, reply with **“hi”** so a team member picks up your conversation.
