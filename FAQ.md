# Tuftingshop FAQ (short)

Based on support tickets from Aug 2025–Aug 2026. For full detail see `TUFTINGSHOP_FAQ.md`.

## Shipping & tracking

**How long does shipping take?**  
We usually ship within 1–2 business days. Delivery is typically 1–5 working days after that, depending on your country. You’ll get a tracking email when the parcel leaves our warehouse in the Netherlands.

**How do I track my order?**  
Use the tracking link in your shipment email, or check the Help Center. Still stuck? Email us with your order number.

**Tracking isn’t moving / says delivered but I don’t have it.**  
Carrier delays happen. Check neighbors and safe places first. Then contact us with your order + tracking number so we can open a tracer or share proof of delivery.

**Can my order arrive in more than one box?**  
Yes. Larger orders (especially yarn) are often split. Each box has its own tracking number.

## Orders, changes & cancellations

**Can I change my address or items?**  
Only **before** the order ships. Email us ASAP with your order number and the change.

**Can I cancel?**  
Yes, if it hasn’t shipped yet. Contact us immediately.

## Customs (outside the EU)

**Does shipping include customs / tariffs?**  
**No.** Import fees, VAT, or duties outside the EU are charged by your country’s customs and are your responsibility. EU prices already include applicable taxes.

## Missing or wrong items

**Something is missing or wrong.**  
Email us your order number, what’s missing/wrong, and photos if possible. We’ll check for a split shipment or packing error and offer a reship, refund, or voucher.

## Returns & refunds

**Can I return tufting cloth?**  
**No** — cloth is cut to order and is non-returnable. See our [refund policy](https://tuftingshop.com/policies/refund-policy).

**Other returns?**  
Unused items in original packaging may be returnable within 14 days of delivery (exclusions apply). Shipping costs for returns are on the customer. Contact [info@tuftingshop.com](mailto:info@tuftingshop.com).

## Discounts

**My code doesn’t work.**  
Check spelling, expiry, and that you’re using the same email the voucher was issued to. Still stuck? Email us a screenshot.

## Contact

Email **info@tuftingshop.com** with your order number. If an auto-reply doesn’t help, reply with **“hi”** so a team member takes over.
